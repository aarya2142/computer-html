1.1 Introduction to computer

Computers have revolutionized the way we calculate, process, and store information. Initially, computers were large machines with immense processing capabilities, designed to handle complex calculations and data processing tasks. Over the past few decades, the evolution of technology has significantly reduced their size while exponentially increasing their power and speed. Modern computers are powerful tools essential for a wide range of applications, from scientific research and data analysis to entertainment and communication. They have become an indispensable part of various industries, aiding in everything from medical diagnostics to financial modeling.

In everyday life, computers play a crucial role in enhancing our productivity and connectivity. Whether we are working in an office, studying, shopping online, or even controlling household appliances, computers facilitate and streamline these activities. The integration of computers into our daily routineshas transformed how we interact with the world, providing us with instant access to information and enabling real-time communication across the globe. Asthology instintas to advance, the impact of computers on our lives will only grow, further cementing their role as a foundational element of modern society.

1.1.1 Computer Definition

A computer is an electronic device that takes input from the user, processes it using a set of instructions called a program, produces output, and stores the result for future use. It consists of hardware and software; hardware refers to to physical components like the processor, memory, monitor, keyboard, and printer, while software is a collection of programs that control the computer and perform tasks. Without software, hardware alone is useless. Computers accept input in the form of data, programs, or user responses - data being raw facts, programs being executable instructions, and responses being user-provided input. The core function of a computer is data processing, which is why it's often called a data processor or data processing system. Apart from this, computers are widely used for typing documents, sending emails, browsing the internet, playing games, and creating or editing spreadsheets, presentations, and videos.

1.1 Introduction to computer

Computers have revolutionized the way we calculate, process, and store information. Initially, computers were large machines with immense processing capabilities, designed to handle complex calculations and data processing tasks. Over the past few decades, the evolution of technology has significantly reduced their size while exponentially increasing their power and speed. Modern computers are powerful tools essential for a wide range of applications, from scientific research and data analysis to entertainment and communication. They have become an indispensable part of various industries, aiding in everything from medical diagnostics to financial modeling. In everyday life, computers play a crucial role in enhancing our productivity and connectivity. Whether we are working in an office, studying, shopping online, or even controlling household appliances, computers facilitate and streamline these activities.
